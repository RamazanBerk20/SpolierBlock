          About

          Everything runs on this device. What you read, what you mark and what gets covered never leaves your browser.

          • Title matchingA catalog of films, series, games and books, with their other names and main characters.
          • Spoiler checkA small language model that tells a spoiler from a mention.
          • ImagesAn image model and text recognition for posters, stills and screenshots.
          • NetworkNone, except catalog updates you switch on or start yourself.
